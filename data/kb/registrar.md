## Registrar's Office contact information
The Registrar's Office can be reached by email at registrar@na.edu or by phone at (832) 230-5555. The mailing address is North American University Registrar's Office, 11929 W Airport Blvd., Stafford, TX 77477. Office hours are not listed on the registrar page.

## How to register for classes (continuing students)
Continuing students should first meet with their Academic Advisor; if the advisor is not available, they should see their Department Chair. After advising, students register for classes through the student portal (portal.na.edu). If online registration is closed, continuing students submit a "Student Course Registration" form to the Registrar's Office before the registration period ends, and the office registers them.

## How to register for classes (new and graduate students)
New students who have transfer credits should see their Department Chair for advising before registering. New students without transfer credits should visit the Registrar's Office to register. Master's (graduate) students should meet with their Academic Advisor before registering.

## How to add or drop a class
To add or drop a course, meet with your Academic Advisor during the Add/Drop period. Complete the "course add/drop" form with your advisor (the form is in the Student Forms section of the portal: https://portal.na.edu/ICS/Students/Forms.jnz) and submit it to the Registrar's Office. Add/Drop dates are published on the academic calendar: https://www.na.edu/academics/academic-calendar/

## Viewing your class schedule and the course schedule
You can view your personal class schedule on the student portal once Technical Support has set up your Office 365 account. The university course schedule is on the NAU website under Academics, then Course Schedule: https://www.na.edu/academics/course-schedule/

## Ordering an official transcript
Transcripts cost $10 each and take 4 business days to process. Printed transcripts are mailed (order at https://pay.na.edu/shop/registrar/fees-under-registrar/transcript-form-request-fee/). Electronic transcripts are emailed through the National Student Clearinghouse (https://tsorder.studentclearinghouse.org/school/ficecode/04179500). Do not open the envelope of a mailed transcript, or it becomes unofficial.

## Holds and transcripts
Students with a hold from any NAU office cannot receive an official transcript. Check your student portal to see your holds, and contact the office that placed the hold to resolve it.

## Verification of enrollment (VOE)
A regular verification of enrollment letter costs $5 and a notarized one costs $20. Processing takes 4 business days. Students with a hold from the Bursar's Office or the Registrar's Office cannot order a VOE.

## FERPA and student records
Under FERPA, students may inspect their educational records by submitting a written request; access is provided within 30 days. Most information beyond directory information is protected. To file a FERPA complaint, use the FERPA Complaint Form: https://www.na.edu/documents/students/Registrar/ferpa_complaint_form.pdf
