## Free tutoring
Free tutoring is available daily from 9 A.M. to 7 P.M. in the Student Success Center for all undergraduate subjects. Through the "Earning through learning" program, students earn points for each tutoring session that can be exchanged for free NAU gear.

## Student Success Center location and hours
The Student Success Center is in Room 830. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Email: success@na.edu. Appointments can be requested through the Student Success page.

## Academic workshops and peer mentoring
The Success Center typically runs four academic workshops per semester. The Peer Mentor Program works with incoming freshmen alongside the freshman seminar course, with weekly mentoring sessions, adviser meetings and networking events.

## Disability accommodations
Students with a disability can request accommodations by completing the disability accommodation request form (https://www.na.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/disability-accommodation-request-form.pdf) and submitting appropriate medical documentation. The Student Success Center determines and arranges the accommodations.
