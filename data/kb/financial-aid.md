## How to apply for financial aid (FAFSA)
Complete the FAFSA every academic year to be considered for federal grants, federal loans and Federal Work-Study. For federal loans you must also complete entrance counseling, sign the Master Promissory Note (MPN), accept the loan, and submit verification documents if requested.

## Federal grants (Pell and FSEOG)
The Federal Pell Grant is for undergraduates who have not yet earned a bachelor's or professional degree and who show exceptional financial need; the amount is based on the FAFSA, the Student Aid Index and enrollment intensity. The Federal Supplemental Educational Opportunity Grant (FSEOG) is for undergraduates with exceptional need; funding is limited and students must be enrolled at least half-time (6 credits). Grants do not have to be repaid.

## Federal student loans
Federal Direct Subsidized Loans are for undergraduates with financial need; the government pays the interest while you are enrolled at least half-time, and annual maximums range from $3,500 to $5,500 depending on class level. Federal Direct Unsubsidized Loans are available to all undergraduates who file a FAFSA, and interest accrues while enrolled. Federal Direct PLUS Loans are for graduate and professional students and for parents of dependent undergraduates.

## Loan deadlines and disbursement
Federal loan requests are processed on a rolling basis during the semester of enrollment. All loan documents must be received by the second-to-last week before the semester ends. Allow at least 2 weeks from loan acceptance for disbursement. Requests received after the final deadlines cannot be guaranteed.

## Private loans
Private loans come from private lenders and may have variable rates, fees and stricter eligibility. Use federal and other aid first, compare offers from your bank or credit union, apply directly with the lender, and complete the Private Loan Self-Certification Form before disbursement. International students need a creditworthy U.S. citizen co-signer for private loans.

## Federal Work-Study
Federal Work-Study (FWS) provides part-time jobs for eligible undergraduate and graduate students with financial need. Apply to positions posted on NAU Student Jobs. Receiving FWS does not guarantee a job, and you must meet Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) to be eligible.

## Pell Grant and adding classes after census date
Pell Grant eligibility is calculated using the semester Census Date. Classes added after the census date do not count toward Pell eligibility, and not attending a course triggers a mandatory recalculation.
