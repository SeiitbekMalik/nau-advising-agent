## International Student Office contact and hours
The International Student Office (ISO) is at 11929 W Airport Blvd, Houston, TX 77477. Phone: (832) 230-5555, then press 2. Email: iso@na.edu (for appointments and for reporting address, name or phone changes). Walk-in hours are Monday to Wednesday, 10:00 am to 3:30 pm; Thursday and Friday are by appointment only.

## Full-time enrollment requirement for F-1 students
F-1 students must enroll full-time every semester: 12 credits at the university level. A reduced course load requires ISO permission and is allowed only for approved medical, academic or last-semester reasons.

## Reporting changes to the ISO
F-1 students must report a change of major to the ISO, and must report a change of address, legal name or phone number within 10 days. Renew your passport about six months before it expires and bring new passports or visas to the ISO to be copied.

## On-campus work for F-1 students
You must get ISO authorization before starting any job. For on-campus work, get a written job offer from the on-campus employer, then visit the ISO for an authorization letter. On-campus work is limited to 20 hours per week during the school year and up to 40 hours per week during breaks. Unauthorized off-campus work is a serious status violation.

## Curricular Practical Training (CPT)
CPT must directly relate to your major and be part of your program of study, and requires one full academic year of study in the U.S. The ISO authorizes CPT together with your student advisor and issues a new I-20. Your employer letter, on letterhead, must include the internship's physical address, start and end dates and a description of duties. Twelve or more months of full-time CPT makes you ineligible for OPT; part-time CPT does not affect OPT.

## Optional Practical Training (OPT)
OPT must relate to your major. You may apply for 12 months of OPT at each education level. The ISO issues a new I-20 with its recommendation and USCIS makes the final decision. File Form I-765 electronically with the fee 60 to 90 days before you want to start working. You cannot start work until you receive your Employment Authorization Document (EAD).

## STEM OPT extension
A 24-month STEM OPT extension may be possible if your current OPT is based on a degree on the official STEM list, your employer uses E-Verify, you have not had a STEM extension before, your DSO issues a new I-20 with the recommendation, and you file Form I-765 with the fee. Report changes in name, address, employer or loss of employment to your DSO within 10 days.

## Travel signature and traveling abroad
Before traveling outside the U.S., get a travel signature from the ISO on your I-20. It is valid for one year but is recommended every six months, and you need it to re-enter the U.S. Carry your original I-20, passport and visa when traveling.

## Extending your I-20
The completion date on your I-20 is an estimate. If you will not finish on time, contact the ISO before your I-20 expires to request an extension; the delay must be for compelling academic or medical reasons.

## Reduced course load for academic difficulties
A reduced course load for academic difficulty requires a letter from your academic advisor. It is allowed for one term per program level, with a minimum of 6 credit hours for undergraduates or 4 credit hours for graduate students, and you must return to full-time enrollment the next term.
