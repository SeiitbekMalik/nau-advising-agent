## IT help desk contact
For technical help, email support@na.edu (preferred: you receive a ticket number, progress updates and a notice when the ticket is closed). You can also call 832-230-5541 or walk in to the 8th floor, Room 820. Office hours are 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM.

## Student accounts and logins
The NAU student portal is at portal.na.edu. Canvas (online courses) is at na.instructure.com. Office 365 is at login.microsoftonline.com and Microsoft 365 apps at m365.cloud.microsoft. NAU email is accessed through outlook.office365.com.

## Resetting your password
To reset your NAU (Microsoft) password, use https://passwordreset.microsoftonline.com/. If that does not work, email support@na.edu to open a help desk ticket.
