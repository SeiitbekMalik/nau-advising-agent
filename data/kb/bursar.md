## Bursar's Office contact information and hours
The Bursar's Office can be reached by email at sa@na.edu (usually answered within an hour) or by phone at 832-230-5155 (main university line 832-230-5555). The Business Office is in Room SC201. The service window is open Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and closed on weekends.

## How to pay tuition
You can check your enrollment and account balance and pay online through MyNAU with American Express, MasterCard or Visa. Payments can also be mailed to North American University, Attn: Business Office, 11929 W Airport Blvd, Stafford, Texas 77477. Bank of America money transfer details are available as a separate document from the Business Office.

## Tuition payment plan
A tuition payment plan is available for students who have difficulty paying tuition in full by the due dates. Details are in the Tuition Payment Plan Policy; visit the Business Office in Room SC201 or call 832-230-5155.

## Refunds of credit balances
Credit balance refunds usually come from schedule changes, overpayment, financial aid or confirmation deposits. Refunds are issued in the student's name regardless of who paid. Accounts with financial aid are refunded within 14 business days after credits exceed charges. Mailed checks may take up to three weeks to arrive. Students can enroll in direct deposit for refunds.

## Tuition refund schedule when you withdraw (fall and spring)
For fall and spring semesters, the tuition refund depends on when you withdraw: on or before the 1st class day, 100%; 2nd to 5th class day, 75%; 6th to 10th class day, 50%; 11th to 15th class day, 25%; after the 15th class day, no refund.

## Tuition refund schedule for summer sessions
Summer 3-week session: 100% on or before the 1st class day, 70% for the 2nd-3rd class days, 25% for the 4th-5th class days, none after the 5th class day. Summer 6-week session: 100% on or before the 1st class day, 75% on the 2nd class day, 50% on the 3rd, 25% on the 4th, none after the 4th class day. Summer 12-week session: 100% on or before the 1st class day, 75% for the 2nd-4th class days, 50% for the 5th-7th, 25% for the 8th-9th, none after the 9th class day.

## If you will not attend your classes
If you will not attend your classes, you must drop or withdraw by the refund deadline. Students are financially and academically responsible for their enrollment.

## What the Bursar's Office handles
The Bursar's Office handles tuition and fee billing, payment plans, FAFSA stipend checks, book vouchers, payments for verification letters and transcripts, application fees, parking tags, and housing and meal plans. It does not make financial aid decisions; those are handled by the Financial Aid Office. The Bursar's Office contacts students through their official NAU email, so check it regularly.
